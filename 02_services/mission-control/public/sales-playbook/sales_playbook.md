# Intro Call Script — Restaurants & Bars (Phuket)

**Purpose:** First cold call to a HoReCa venue. Goal — book a 15–20 min in-person meeting to walk through the catalog. Not a tasting (tastings are coordinated through Bangkok).

**Memory anchors:** *Abrau Durso sparkling / Barrister gin / Indigenous reds.* Plus the Russian-tourist hook.

---

## Opening (10 sec)

Hi, this is [name] from Wine & Whiskey. We're the exclusive distributor of Russian wines, sparkling, and spirits on Phuket. I'll be quick — just calling to introduce ourselves.

## Why we're calling (15 sec)

We're not asking you to switch your main supplier. What we offer is an addition to your list — exclusive Russian labels nobody else on the island can supply. They work two ways: as a cocktail base, or as standalone pours by the glass or bottle.

## What's in the range (30 sec)

— **Sparkling** — a broad lineup from Abrau Durso, the most famous winery in Russia. Works as a Prosecco alternative at the entry level, and as a Crémant alternative a tier up. Noticeably better price point.

— **Gin** — Barrister, London-quality, several flavor profiles, great for cocktails. Quality is Bombay-plus, price is Beefeater-minus.

— **Whites** — light styles, including a solid Riesling.

— **Reds** — indigenous Russian grapes, like Krasnostop. Something genuinely different for guests looking for a story.

## The Russian-tourist angle (15 sec)

One more thing worth mentioning — Phuket has a huge Russian guest flow. A familiar label on your list is often what closes the sale for them. That's a margin opportunity most venues here don't tap into.

## Close (10 sec)

Can I drop by for 15–20 minutes to walk you through the catalog and pricing? If something catches your eye, we'll coordinate samples as a next step. What works better — early week or end of week?

---

## Cheat sheet — if they ask

- **"Why Russian?"** — Quality has come a long way. In sparkling and gin specifically, we offer the best price/quality on the island. Plus you get exclusivity — these labels aren't available through any other distributor here.
- **"Licensing / import?"** — Fully legal, official import, full paperwork. Happy to show documents when we meet.
- **"Send me the price list"** — Will do. But the price list alone won't tell you much — the labels are unfamiliar. A 15-minute meeting is more useful, so I can show you which SKUs fit your concept.
- **"Can we taste first?"** — Tastings are coordinated through our Bangkok office, so I'd want to do them properly — pick the right SKUs for your venue first, then arrange a session.
