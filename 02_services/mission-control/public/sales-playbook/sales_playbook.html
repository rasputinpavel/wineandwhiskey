WINE & WHISKEY
Sales · HoReCa Intro Call Script
Phuket
For sales reps · Cold call to restaurants & bars

First call.
15 seconds to get attention.

Goal — book a 15–20 minute in-person meeting to walk through the catalog. Not a tasting. Tastings are coordinated through Bangkok and arranged after the venue picks SKUs.

Memory anchors Abrau Durso sparkling · Barrister gin · Indigenous reds · Russian-tourist hook

Positioning We are an addition to their list — not a replacement for their main supplier. Exclusive lineup. Nobody else on the island carries it.

01
Opening
10 sec

"Hi, this is [name] from Wine & Whiskey. We're the exclusive distributor of Russian wines, sparkling, and spirits on Phuket. I'll be quick — just calling to introduce ourselves."

02
Why we're calling
15 sec

"We're not asking you to switch your main supplier. What we offer is an addition to your list — exclusive Russian labels nobody else on the island can supply. They work two ways: as a cocktail base, or as standalone pours by the glass or bottle."

03
What's in the range
30 sec
Sparkling
A broad lineup from Abrau Durso, the most famous winery in Russia. Works as a Prosecco alternative at the entry level, and as a Crémant alternative a tier up. Noticeably better price point.
Gin
Barrister, London-quality, several flavor profiles, built for cocktails. Quality is Bombay-plus, price is Beefeater-minus.
Whites
Light styles, including a solid Riesling.
Reds
Indigenous Russian grapes, like Krasnostop. Something genuinely different for guests looking for a story.
04
The Russian-tourist angle
15 sec

"One more thing worth mentioning — Phuket has a huge Russian guest flow. A familiar label on your list is often what closes the sale for them. That's a margin opportunity most venues here don't tap into."

05
Close
10 sec

"Can I drop by for 15–20 minutes to walk you through the catalog and pricing? If something catches your eye, we'll coordinate samples as a next step. What works better — early week or end of week?"

Reference

If they ask.

Short, calm answers. Don't argue. Bring it back to the meeting.
"Why Russian?"
Quality has come a long way. In sparkling and gin specifically, we offer the best price/quality on the island. Plus you get exclusivity — these labels aren't available through any other distributor here.
"Licensing / import?"
Fully legal, official import, full paperwork. Happy to show documents when we meet.
"Just send me the price list."
Will do. But the price list alone won't tell you much — the labels are unfamiliar. A 15-minute meeting is more useful, so I can show you which SKUs fit your concept.
"Can we taste first?"
Tastings are coordinated through our Bangkok office, so I'd want to do them properly — pick the right SKUs for your venue first, then arrange a session.
