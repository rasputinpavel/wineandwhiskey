Why it works for your program

Built for cocktails, not the bottle wall

Botanical ratios are tuned for mixing. The flavoured SKUs hold their character through dilution and ice, where most "flavoured gins" disappear after the first stir.

Margin that respects the bar

Wholesale ~25–35% below the Bombay Sapphire benchmark at comparable quality. Run a premium pour price without giving up GP%.

Four expressions, one SKU family

Single supplier covers your well plus your signature menu. Less back-bar clutter, fewer POs, cleaner training for bar staff.

Signature-cocktail leverage

Each flavoured expression is a ready-made signature serve — basil gimlet, hibiscus negroni, cucumber Tom Collins — without in-house infusion work.
