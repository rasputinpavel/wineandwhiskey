## Why it works for your program

- **Built for cocktails, not for the bottle wall.** Botanical ratios are tuned for mixing — the flavoured SKUs hold their character through dilution and ice, where most "flavoured gins" disappear after the first stir.
- **Margin that respects the bar.** Wholesale ~25–35% below the Bombay Sapphire benchmark at comparable quality, which means you can run a premium pour price without giving up GP%.
- **Five expressions, one SKU family.** A single supplier covers your well + your signature menu. Less line clutter on the back bar, fewer purchase orders, cleaner training for bar staff.
- **Multiple flavours = signature cocktail leverage.** Each flavoured expression is a ready-made signature serve — a basil gimlet, a hibiscus negroni, a cucumber Tom Collins — without the bartender having to infuse in-house.
