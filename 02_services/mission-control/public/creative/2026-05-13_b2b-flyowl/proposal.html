Who we are.
Why this list works.

Wine & Whiskey is a Phuket wine boutique. We curate every bottle we sell — small-producer wines, premium spirits, the categories that hold a rooftop floor. Our list is built around producers we’ve tasted and importers we work with directly. For FlyO we drafted a focused selection: rooftop-friendly whites and sparklings, anchor reds for the meat side of the menu, a tight spirits program, and a pairing map for your current dishes.
