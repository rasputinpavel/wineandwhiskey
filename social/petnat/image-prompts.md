# Pét-Nat Slides — Image Prompts (NBP)

Backgrounds for Instagram Stories (9:16). Text overlay sits in the **bottom 40%** of each frame
— that zone must fade to near-black so white type stays readable. Upper 60% carries all the visual weight.

All slides: dark mode · photorealistic · no faces · no full bodies · no text in image.

---

## SLIDE 01 — Hook
*"Older than Champagne."*

```
Editorial product photography background for a wine Instagram Stories slide.

Subject: Extreme macro shot of sparkling wine bubbles rising through golden-amber
liquid. Fill the entire frame with the liquid — no glass edges visible, pure
abstraction. Bubbles vary in size from tiny specks to large globes (2–8mm),
each catching a warm backlight that makes them glow like amber gemstones.
The liquid itself is a deep golden-honey color #C9A84C shifting to deep
burgundy-garnet at the edges.

Lighting: Single warm light source from the upper-right, slightly behind the
liquid, creating a translucent backlit glow through the bubbles. No cold light.
Deep, saturated shadows between bubble clusters.

Composition: Upper 60% of the frame — densely packed, luminous bubbles,
maximum visual richness. Lower 40% — bubbles thin out, liquid deepens
progressively to #1A1A1A matte black by the very bottom, creating a natural
fade zone for text overlay. No hard vignette — the transition is organic,
like depth.

Mood: intimate, cinematic, otherworldly. Like looking through a porthole
into something alive.
Photorealistic, ultra-detailed, no illustration, no cartoon.
Format: 9:16
```

## SLIDE 02 — History
*"Born by accident · 1531 · France"*

```
Editorial atmospheric photography background for a wine Instagram Stories slide.

Subject: Ancient French wine cellar interior. Stone archway visible in upper
portion, rough-hewn limestone walls with centuries of moisture staining.
Several wine bottles lying horizontally in a stone rack, their glass covered
in fine dust and cellar patina. The bottles are dark glass, anonymous — no
labels readable.

Lighting: Single warm amber flame source (candle or oil lantern) positioned
to the right, slightly out of frame. The flame creates dramatic directional
light — stone textures glow warmly on one side, deep shadow on the other.
The light catches dust particles floating in the air. No overhead light.
Amber-gold glow on glass surfaces matching #C9A84C.

Composition: Upper 60% — the cellar scene in full richness: stone arch,
bottles, flickering amber light, dust particles suspended in air. Lower 40%
— the floor of the cellar, unlit flagstones, fading smoothly to #1A1A1A
near-black at frame bottom. The transition is shadow, not fade.

Atmosphere: Centuries of silence. Cold stone, warm flame. Something discovered
by accident in this very darkness.
Photorealistic, editorial style, no faces, no people.
Format: 9:16
```

## SLIDE 03 — Beer cap
*"Beer cap. Not cork."*

```
Editorial product photography background for a wine Instagram Stories slide.

Subject: Extreme close-up of a wine bottle neck and crown cap (metal beer-style
cap), shot from a low angle looking up. The bottle contains rosé sparkling wine
— pink-ruby liquid visible through the glass. At the crown cap, foam and small
wine droplets cling to the metal and glass, suggesting the bottle was just
opened or shaken. The crown cap itself is slightly askew, caught in the moment
of removal, its ridged metal edge catching warm light.

Glass texture: condensation droplets on the lower bottle neck, glass refracting
the pink liquid into rich color. Amber-gold highlights (#C9A84C) on the metal
cap ridges and glass shoulder.

Lighting: Single warm key light from the left at 45°, creating a strong
shadow on the right side of the bottle. The background is pure matte #1A1A1A.
The light makes the pink foam glow and the metal cap gleam with warm amber.

Composition: The bottle neck and crown cap occupy the upper-center 60% of the
frame — dynamic, slightly tilted angle, not perfectly vertical. Lower 40%:
pure #1A1A1A darkness, a few stray droplets on the surface below, fading
completely to black for text zone.

Mood: raw, unfiltered, deliberately anti-refined. The beauty of the imperfect.
Photorealistic, ultra-detailed glass and liquid, no faces.
Format: 9:16
```

## SLIDE 04 — Comparison
*Pure texture background — no focal subject*

```
Minimalist editorial background for a wine Instagram Stories slide.
This image has no focal subject — it is pure surface and atmosphere.

Subject: Matte dark surface — black slate or fine-grain anthracite stone,
shot straight-on. The texture is subtle: micro-grain, tiny natural pores,
not polished, not shiny. Color is #1A1A1A to #222222, with barely perceptible
warm undertone (not cold). A ghost of a shadow crosses diagonally from upper-
left — as if from a wine glass stem not in frame, so faint it might not be
there at all.

Lighting: Extremely raking light from the far left at near-horizontal angle,
just enough to reveal the micro-texture of the stone. No highlights, no gloss.
The entire frame is dark — #1A1A1A to #2a2a2a — with the texture as the
only visual event.

The bottom half is effectively pure black. The top half has the faintest
texture readable.

No bottles. No glass. No liquid. No objects of any kind.
Just the surface, breathing.
Photorealistic material study.
Format: 9:16
```

## SLIDE 05 — Reveal
*"Pét-Nat. Natural. Unfiltered. Alive."*

```
Editorial product photography background for a wine Instagram Stories slide.

Subject: Three sparkling wine bottles with crown caps (no corks, no wire cages)
standing on a dark matte surface. Each bottle contains different liquid:
— Left bottle: cloudy white/pale gold, slightly translucent and hazy
— Center bottle: deep rosé pink, vivid and saturated
— Right bottle: dark ruby-garnet, almost opaque, rich red

The bottles are arranged in a casual, slightly uneven line — center bottle
slightly taller or pushed forward, the others at natural offset. Crown caps
visible at the top. No labels readable. The glass is simple, contemporary
bottle shape.

Lighting: Warm key light from the left at 45°, creating strong lateral
illumination. The light catches the liquid colors through the glass —
the white bottle glows pale gold, the rosé blazes pink, the red deepens
to garnet. Amber-gold highlights (#C9A84C) on all three crown caps and
glass shoulders. Deep shadows fall to the right and behind.

Surface: Dark matte slate or stone, #1A1A1A. A faint warm reflection of
each bottle's liquid color pools on the surface below each bottle.

Composition: All three bottles occupy the upper 55% of the frame, leaving
clear dark surface below. The lower 45% is the surface fading to pure black
— clean space for text overlay. The arrangement feels like a bar counter
after closing time: casual, honest, no performance.

Mood: alive, confident, quietly proud. Three characters.
Photorealistic, no faces, no hands, no people.
Format: 9:16
```

## Usage notes

- Each prompt generates a **background only** — no text in the image
- Drop into the corresponding slide in [slides.html](slides.html), replace the `.ph` placeholder div
- For slide 04, the image is intentionally almost invisible — the layout is the content
- Slide 01 can also work as a loop video (Reels cover) if generated as motion
