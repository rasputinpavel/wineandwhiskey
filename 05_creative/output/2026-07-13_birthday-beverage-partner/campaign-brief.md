# Birthday Beverage-Partner — Campaign Brief

**Date:** 2026-07-13
**Status:** Wave-1 launch kit
**Owner:** Pavel
**Platform:** Meta (Instagram / Facebook)

---

## 1. Positioning

**"Your beverage partner for the celebration."**

Not a birthday discount. The offer is a **party sourcing / curation service**:
tell us your budget and format, we curate the most relevant set of beverages
(alcoholic **and** non-alcoholic), and order + deliver them to your celebration.
It's light wholesale — the bigger the order, the better the price.

**Desired action:** a WhatsApp conversation with the party brief (date, guest
count, budget, alcoholic/non-alcoholic preferences). This is lead-gen / message
generation — **not** a storefront reservation.

## 2. Legal & platform guardrails (non-negotiable)

Thailand's Alcoholic Beverage Control Act restricts alcohol advertising, and this
offer (sourcing + delivery of drinks) is more sensitive than a plain image ad.
Meta's alcohol policy also applies.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Lead from | Celebration, curation, delivery convenience — **never** bottles, brands, pouring, or consumption |
| Frame | Everything as **"beverages, with and without alcohol"** — this is the legal shield |
| Never | Alcohol brand names, pouring/drinking imagery or verbs, benefit claims (alcohol improves social/sexual/professional success or solves problems) |
| Age-gate | **20+** (Thailand legal drinking age); can raise to 25+ on request |
| Meta setting | Alcohol-policy flag **enabled** on every ad set |
| Thai + alcohol | Highest legal risk → **excluded from Wave-1** |

## 3. Campaign map

Objective: **Messages (Click-to-WhatsApp)**. Structure split by language so
budgets and stats don't mix.

| Campaign | Ad set | Targeting | Geo | Age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BD \| RU` | A1 — Birthday-precise | Meta "Upcoming birthday" | Phuket | 20+ |
| `BD \| RU` | A2 — Planners / broad | Broad, no birthday flag | Phuket | 20+ |
| `BD \| EN` | A1 — Birthday-precise | Meta "Upcoming birthday" | Phuket | 20+ |
| `BD \| EN` | A2 — Planners / broad | Broad, no birthday flag | Phuket | 20+ |

**Platform reality — accept this:** Meta has **no** native "birthday next month"
targeting. The nearest available option is *Upcoming birthday (within ~1 week)*.
So the month-ahead reach comes from the **creative** ("Birthday coming up? Start
planning now"), not from targeting — while A1 captures the hot, near-birthday
segment that Meta's native flag does support.
