Why it works for your program

Cocktail base that doesn't fight the build

Clean acidity, low residual sugar — works in spritzes, French 75 variants, sparkling sangria and welcome cocktails without throwing the balance.

By-the-glass economics

Wholesale per-bottle cost lands ~20–30% below comparable Prosecco DOC and well below Champagne. A 150 ml pour holds margin even at promotional menu prices.

Bottle service that travels

Pale salmon colour photographs well in the sun. Cork-and-cage presentation reads premium on a beach-club table or yacht ice bucket.

Méthode traditionnelle, not tank

Secondary fermentation in bottle — finer mousse, longer hold in the glass, closer to Champagne texture than to Prosecco.
