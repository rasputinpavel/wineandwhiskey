01 Consignment & Title

1.1 The Consignor delivers the products listed in Appendix A (the "Goods") on a consignment basis. The Consignee does not purchase the Goods on delivery.

1.2 Title to each item remains with the Consignor until that item is sold to an end customer. Title passes to the Consignee at the moment of sale, triggering the payment obligation in Section 5.

1.3 The Goods are held by the Consignee as bailee, kept separately identifiable as the Consignor's property, and shall not be pledged, encumbered, or used as security.

02 Term

2.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature and continues on an ongoing basis until terminated by either Party under Section 9.

2.2 The Parties may review the arrangement at any time and adjust the working stock, the consigned range, or pricing (Appendix A) by written agreement, without affecting the validity of this Agreement.

03 Delivery & Replenishment

3.1 Each delivery is accompanied by a Delivery Note itemising product, quantity, and consignment price. The Consignee signs it on receipt; the signed Delivery Note is the record of Goods on hand.

3.2 Replenishment is made as needed — typically alongside the weekly reconciliation — to keep an agreed working stock, confirmed by the Consignor and recorded on a new Delivery Note.

3.3 The Consignee provides secure storage suitable for wine and spirits (conditions if relevant) and is responsible for the Goods while on its premises.

04 Weekly Reporting & Reconciliation

4.1 Every Tuesday the Consignee sends a list of items sold since the previous report (the "Weekly Sales Report" — see Appendix B), covering the period through the preceding day.

4.2 On the same day the Parties reconcile delivered vs. sold vs. on hand:

Opening + Delivered − Sold − Loss = Closing stock

4.3 Discrepancies are resolved at the weekly reconciliation. Missing or damaged Goods that cannot be accounted for are treated as sold for payment purposes (Section 5), unless agreed otherwise in writing.

05 Pricing, Invoicing & Payment

5.1 Consignment price. The Consignor's price per item is set in Appendix A, VAT (7%) inclusive. The Consignee sets its own resale price; the difference is its margin.

5.2 Tax Invoice. Against each Weekly Sales Report the Consignor issues a Tax Invoice for the items sold that week, at the consignment price.

5.3 Payment. The Consignee pays within 7 days of invoice, by bank transfer to: K Bank · a/c 150-3-12001-8 · New Latitude Co., Ltd.

5.4 Receipt. On receipt of payment the Consignor issues a Receipt for the amount paid.

5.5 Document flow per cycle:

Delivery Note→ Weekly Sales Report (Tue)→ Tax Invoice→ Payment→ Receipt

06 Responsibility for the Goods

6.1 From signed receipt until sale or return, the Consignee bears the risk of loss, theft, breakage, or deterioration on its premises, and shall pay the consignment price for any Goods so lost or damaged.

6.2 The Consignee shall store, handle, and display the Goods with reasonable care and in compliance with applicable licensing and alcohol-sale regulations.

07 Licences & Compliance

7.1 The Consignee warrants it holds all licences and permits required to store, display, and sell alcoholic beverages, and shall comply with all applicable Thai laws, including sale-hour restrictions and the prohibition on sale to minors.

08 Return of Unsold Goods

8.1 On expiry or termination, the Consignee returns all unsold Goods in resaleable condition. A closing reconciliation is performed; the Consignee pays for any Goods sold but not yet invoiced, and for any unaccounted or non-resaleable Goods at the consignment price.

8.2 Return transport / collection is arranged by Consignor / Consignee.

09 Term & Termination

9.1 Either Party may terminate at any time on 7 days' written notice.

9.2 Either Party may terminate immediately on the other's material breach (including non-payment) not cured within __ days of written notice.

9.3 On termination, Sections 5 (for sold Goods), 6, 8, 10, and 11 survive.

10 Confidentiality

Pricing, terms, and commercial information exchanged under this Agreement are confidential and shall not be disclosed to third parties without the other Party's consent, except as required by law.

11 General

11.1 Independent parties. Nothing here creates a partnership, employment, or agency beyond the consignment described.

11.2 Entire agreement. This Agreement with its Appendices is the entire agreement and supersedes prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed.

11.3 Governing law. Governed by the laws of Thailand; disputes subject to the courts of Phuket.
