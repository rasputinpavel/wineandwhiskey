# Sales Representative Agreement — Trial Period

This agreement is made between:

- **Wine & Whiskey** _(legal entity TBD — to be filled in before signing)_, hereinafter "the **Company**"
- **Benz** _(full legal name TBD)_, Thai national, ID/Passport No. _TBD_, hereinafter "the **Representative**"

## 1. Term

This agreement is effective from **10 May 2026** to **11 June 2026** (a one-month trial period). Before **8 June 2026**, the parties will jointly review the results and decide whether to extend, convert into a permanent engagement, or terminate.

## 2. Scope of work

The Representative will identify and meet decision-makers (owners, F&B managers, buyers) at restaurants, bars, cafes, hotels, and other B2B venues in Phuket, in order to introduce and promote the Company's portfolio of **wines and spirits**.

## 3. Compensation

### 3.1 Per-meeting fee

- **THB 1,000** for each qualified first meeting.
- A meeting **qualifies** when it is (a) held in person, (b) with an authorized decision-maker (owner, F&B manager, or buyer), and (c) logged in the shared tracker provided by the Company, including: date, venue, contact name, contact role.
- Meetings not logged in the tracker do not qualify for payment.
- Per-meeting fees are paid **weekly on Fridays**, in THB.

### 3.2 Commission on first order

- **10%** of the first paid order from any account first introduced by the Representative during the trial period.
- Commission is calculated on the order amount **net of VAT**.
- Commission is paid within **7 days after the Company has received full payment** from the client.
- Commission applies to the **first paid order only**. No commission on subsequent orders or reorders.
- If the client does not pay the Company, no commission is due.

### 3.3 Withholding tax

The Company will withhold **3% withholding tax** from all payments to the Representative as required for services rendered by an individual under Thai tax law, and will issue ภ.ง.ด. 3 certificates as needed.

## 4. Tracker

All meetings, contacts, and accounts are logged in the shared Google Sheet provided by the Company. The Representative is responsible for keeping the tracker up to date in real time. The tracker is the single source of truth for compensation calculations.

## 5. Confidentiality

Pricing, supplier information, internal margins, and customer lists shared by the Company are confidential and may not be disclosed to third parties during or after this engagement.

## 6. Independent contractor

The Representative provides services as an independent contractor. This agreement does not create an employment relationship. The Representative is responsible for her own transport, equipment, and personal expenses, except as expressly agreed in writing.

The Company will provide product samples from stock as reasonably needed for tasting at meetings, at no cost to the Representative.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with **7 days' written notice** (email or message). Per-meeting fees earned up to the termination date will be paid out. Commissions on orders for which the client has not yet paid the Company at the time of termination are not payable after termination.

## 8. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Thailand.
