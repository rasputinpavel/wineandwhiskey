# Compensation — Benz

## Trial period: 2026-05-10 → 2026-06-11

### Per-meeting fee

- **THB 1,000** per qualified first meeting.
- Qualified = in-person meeting with decision-maker (owner / F&B manager / buyer), logged in tracker (date, venue, contact name, role).
- Paid **weekly on Fridays** in THB.
- Meeting not in tracker → not paid.

### Commission on first order

- **10%** of the first paid order from any account Benz opened during the trial.
- Calculated on **net of VAT** (revenue, not margin).
- Paid **within 7 days after the client has paid Wine & Whiskey in full**.
- **Only the first order** — no commission on reorders.
- If client doesn't pay → no commission.

### Withholding tax

- 3% WHT удерживаем при выплате (стандарт для услуг физлица в Thailand).
- Выдаём ภ.ง.ด. 3 при необходимости.
