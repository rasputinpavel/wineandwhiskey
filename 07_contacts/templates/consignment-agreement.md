> arrangement with Brewery: goods placed on consignment, weekly sales collection every
> Tuesday, replenishment as needed. Replace every `{{placeholder}}`, delete the guidance
> blockquotes, fill Appendix A (consigned goods) and review with the partner before signing.

### 1. Consignment & title

1.1 The Consignor delivers the products listed in **Appendix A** (the "Goods") to the Consignee
on a **consignment** basis. The Consignee does **not** purchase the Goods on delivery.

1.2 **Title** to each item of the Goods remains with the Consignor until that item is **sold**
by the Consignee to an end customer. Title to sold items passes to the Consignee at the moment
of sale, triggering the payment obligation in Section 5.

1.3 The Goods are held by the Consignee as bailee. The Consignee shall keep the Goods separately
identifiable as the Consignor's property and shall not pledge, encumber, or use them as security.

### 2. Trial period

2.1 This Agreement runs for a **trial period of two (2) months** from `{{start date}}` to `{{end date}}`
(the "Trial Period").

2.2 No later than `{{one week}}` before the end of the Trial Period the Parties will review results
and decide whether to (a) continue on a rolling basis, (b) sign a longer-term agreement, or
(c) end the arrangement and return unsold Goods per Section 8.

2.3 If neither Party gives notice and deliveries/reporting continue past the end date, the
Agreement continues month-to-month on the same terms until terminated under Section 9.

### 3. Delivery & replenishment

3.1 Each delivery is accompanied by a **Delivery Note** itemising product, quantity, and
consignment (reference) price. The Consignee signs the Delivery Note on receipt to confirm
quantities. The signed Delivery Note is the record of Goods on hand.

3.2 **Replenishment** is made as needed — typically alongside the weekly reconciliation
(Section 4) — to keep an agreed working stock. Replenishment quantities are confirmed by the
Consignor and recorded on a new Delivery Note.

3.3 The Consignee provides suitable, secure storage appropriate to wine and spirits
(`{{temperature / conditions if relevant}}`) and is responsible for the Goods while on its premises.

### 4. Weekly sales reporting & reconciliation

4.1 **Every Tuesday** the Consignee sends the Consignor a **list of items sold** since the previous
report (the "Weekly Sales Report" — see Appendix B), covering the period through the preceding day.

4.2 On the same day the Parties **reconcile**: Goods delivered (signed Delivery Notes) minus Goods
sold (Weekly Sales Reports) minus any breakage/loss must equal Goods physically on hand.
`Opening stock + Delivered − Sold − Loss = Closing stock.`

4.3 Any discrepancy is investigated and resolved at the weekly reconciliation. Missing or damaged
Goods that cannot be accounted for are treated as **sold** for payment purposes (Section 5),
unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

### 5. Pricing, invoicing & payment

5.1 **Consignment price.** The Consignor's price to the Consignee per item is set in Appendix A
(the "Consignment Price"), VAT (7%) inclusive. The Consignee is free to set its own resale price
to end customers; the difference is the Consignee's margin.

5.2 **Tax Invoice.** Against each Weekly Sales Report the Consignor issues a **Tax Invoice** for the
items sold that week, at the Consignment Price.

5.3 **Payment.** The Consignee pays the Tax Invoice within `{{__ days, e.g. 7}}` of issue, by
bank transfer to: **K Bank** · a/c **150-3-12001-8** · New Latitude Co., Ltd.

5.4 **Receipt.** Upon receipt of payment the Consignor issues a **Receipt** for the amount paid.

5.5 **Document flow per cycle:** Delivery Note (on delivery) → Weekly Sales Report (Tuesday) →
Tax Invoice (for sold items) → payment → Receipt.

### 6. Responsibility for the Goods

6.1 From signed receipt until sale or return, the Consignee is responsible for the Goods and bears
the risk of loss, theft, breakage, or deterioration while on its premises, and shall pay the
Consignment Price for any Goods so lost or damaged.

6.2 The Consignee shall store, handle, and display the Goods with reasonable care and in compliance
with applicable licensing and alcohol-sale regulations at its premises.

### 7. Licences & compliance

7.1 The Consignee warrants it holds all licences and permits required to store, display, and sell
alcoholic beverages at its premises and shall comply with all applicable Thai laws, including
restrictions on sale hours and sale to minors.

### 8. Return of unsold Goods

8.1 On expiry or termination, the Consignee returns all unsold Goods to the Consignor in
**resaleable condition**. A closing reconciliation is performed; the Consignee pays for any Goods
sold but not yet invoiced/paid, and for any unaccounted or non-resaleable Goods at the
Consignment Price.

8.2 Return transport / collection is arranged by `{{Consignor / Consignee}}`.

### 9. Term & termination

9.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time on `{{__ days, e.g. 7}}` written notice.

9.2 Either Party may terminate immediately on the other's material breach not cured within
`{{__ days}}` of written notice (including non-payment).

9.3 On termination, Sections 5 (for sold Goods), 6, 8, 10, and 11 survive.

### 10. Confidentiality

Pricing, terms, and commercial information exchanged under this Agreement are confidential and
shall not be disclosed to third parties without the other Party's consent, except as required by law.

### 11. General

11.1 **Independent parties.** Nothing here creates a partnership, employment, or agency beyond the
consignment described.

11.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement (with its Appendices) is the entire agreement and
supersedes prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed.

11.3 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of Thailand. Disputes are subject
to the courts of `{{Phuket}}`.
